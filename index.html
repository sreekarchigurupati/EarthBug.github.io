I work at the intersection of brains and backprop — neuroimaging on one side, optimization on the other. Most days you'll catch me coaxing brain scans into tensors, watching a loss curve inch toward its minimum, or arguing with a model that refuses to converge before the next epoch. Most nights, classical fusion is playing somewhere in the background.

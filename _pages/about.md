<section class="about-section reveal">
  <h2>Hi 👋</h2>
  <p>
    I'm a PhD student working at the intersection of <strong>neuroscience and artificial intelligence</strong>. I'm fascinated by how brains learn from sparse, noisy data — and by how we might teach machines to do the same in ways that are interpretable, efficient, and a little more honest about what they don't know.
  </p>
  <p>
    Most days you'll catch me staring at learning curves, scribbling on whiteboards, or arguing with a notebook that refuses to converge. Most nights, classical fusion is playing somewhere in the background.
  </p>
</section>
